Graph surgery DSL

Rune

Rewrites ONNX, TFLite and torch.export graphs with rules you can read. Write the pattern and the change; Rune checks it, applies it and reports what changed.

  • Rules, not scripts — a few lines each
  • A gate refuses anything that would corrupt the graph
  • Proven, or refused: every rewrite is checked
  • A report per run: before, after, what changed
