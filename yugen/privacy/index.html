Privacy policy

Yugen and Yugen Plus, from Byteinfer Labs. Last updated 18 October 2026.

Byteinfer Labs does not collect, store or sell any data from Yugen or Yugen Plus, and the apps run no server of their own.

What stays on your phone

Your widgets, pages, themes, settings, reminders and automations stay on your phone, and Android backups of the app are turned off. The apps read your calendar, your notifications and your list of installed apps on the phone to show them in widgets.

What other services receive

Some features send data to other services when you use them.

FeatureSent toWhat is sent
Setting up LinuxPublic Arch Linux mirrorsDownload requests, which carry your network address
Weather widgetwttr.inYour approximate location, rounded to about one kilometre, when you allow location access

Voice input (Yugen Plus)Your phone’s speech recognizerYour speech, which the recognizer turns into text

What an agent may read

An agent reads your calendar, notifications or other phone data only when you allow it on Yugen’s permissions screen. In Yugen Plus, the agent uses Android’s accessibility service only for a task you start, shows each step with a Stop button, and asks before anything that sends, pays or deletes.

Removing your data

You can remove Linux and the agents from Yugen’s settings at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.
