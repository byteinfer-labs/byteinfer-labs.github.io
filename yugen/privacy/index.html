Privacy policy

Yugen and Yugen Plus, from Byteinfer Labs. Last updated 19 October 2026.

Byteinfer Labs does not collect, store or sell any data from Yugen or Yugen Plus, and the apps run no server of their own.

What stays on your phone

Your widgets, pages, themes, settings, reminders and automations stay on your phone, and Android backups of the app are turned off. The apps read your calendar, your notifications and your list of installed apps on the phone to show them in widgets.

What an agent may read

An agent reads phone data only when you allow it on Yugen’s permissions screen, and what it reads goes to its provider with your messages. In Yugen, the agent can read the list of your installed apps, your approximate location, and the battery and device state. In Yugen Plus, it can also read your notifications and the screens of the apps it operates during a task, which can include messages and contacts, and any screenshot you attach.

In Yugen Plus, the agent uses Android’s accessibility service only for a task you start, shows each step with a Stop button, and asks before anything that sends, pays or deletes. Your calendar is read on the phone for the clock widget and is not sent anywhere.

Removing your data

You can remove Linux and the agents from Yugen’s settings at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.
