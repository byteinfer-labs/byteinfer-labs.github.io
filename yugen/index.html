Yugen

Imagine it. It’s on your home screen.

Yugen is an Android home screen that your AI agent builds. You describe a widget, a page or a whole look, and the agent makes it and places it while you watch.

Coming soon to Google Play

Works with your Claude, ChatGPT, opencode or pi.

Ask, and it appears

The agent card sits on the home screen. You type what you want, and the agent writes the widget, checks it on the screen and tells you what it made.

Arrange it by hand

Hold anywhere to edit. Drag widgets and apps from the drawer, pull an edge to resize, and add or move pages. A theme restyles every widget at once.

Two editions

Both run the agent inside the app, in a small Linux system that downloads about 130 MB when you choose to set it up. Each agent needs your own account with its provider.
