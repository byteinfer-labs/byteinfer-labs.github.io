Yugen

Imagine it. It’s on your home screen.

An Android home screen that your AI agent builds. You describe a widget, a page or a whole look, and the agent makes it and places it while you watch.

Coming soon to Google Play

Works with your Claude, ChatGPT, opencode or pi.

What it does

Ask for it, or make it by hand

Every screen here is real, from Yugen running on a phone. Drag the ring, or use the arrows.

By hand, too

Arrange it with your fingers

Hold anywhere on the home screen and it turns into an editor.

  • Drag and resizeA drop shape shows where a widget will land, and pulling an edge makes it larger.
  • Apps from the drawerHold an app and carry it onto any page, or into a widget.
  • Pages in any orderAdd a page, move it left or right, or delete it.
Yugen Plus

The agent uses your phone for you

Ask for something done in your other apps. The agent opens them, reads the screen and taps through each step while a pill at the top shows what it is doing, with a Stop button. It asks you before anything that sends, pays or deletes.

Phone tasks
Alarms, messages, places in Maps and settings, done in the apps themselves.
Make something new
One sentence becomes a widget, an automation or a theme, and waits in your inbox for approval.
Smarter automations
Start one when an app opens, or let it ask the agent.
Suggestions, voice, memory
Ideas from how you use your phone, spoken tasks, and what you told it before.

Two editions

Yugen

Free

  • Widgets, pages and themes your agent builds
  • Editing by touch, and apps dragged from the drawer
  • Reminders and automations

Yugen Plus

Paid

  • Everything in Yugen
  • Phone tasks in your other apps, with a Stop button
  • Make something new, with an inbox to approve it
  • Automations that start when an app opens or ask the agent
  • Suggestions, voice input and memory

Both run the agent in a small Linux system inside the app, which downloads about 130 MB when you choose to set it up. Each agent needs your own account with its provider.
