Yugen

Imagine it. It’s on your home screen.

Yugen is an Android home screen that your AI agent builds. You describe a widget, a page or a whole look, and the agent makes it and places it while you watch.

Coming soon to Google Play

Works with your Claude, ChatGPT, opencode or pi.

Ask, and it appears

The agent card sits on the home screen. You type what you want, and the agent writes the widget, checks it on the screen and tells you what it made.

Arrange it by hand

Hold anywhere to edit. Drag widgets and apps from the drawer, pull an edge to resize, and add or move pages. A theme restyles every widget at once.

Yugen Plus: the agent uses your phone

In Yugen Plus you can ask the agent to do things in your other apps. It opens them, reads the screen and taps through the steps while a card shows each one, with a Stop button, and it asks you before anything that sends, pays or deletes.

Phone tasks
“Set an alarm for 6:30 called Gym.” “Text Riya that I’m running late.” “Find the cheapest cake on Zomato that delivers now.”
Make something new
One sentence becomes a widget, an automation or a theme, and it waits in your inbox until you approve it.
Smarter automations
Automations can start when you open an app, and they can ask the agent, such as summing up a bank alert into your spending notes.
Suggestions
Twice a day or every three hours, the agent looks at how you use your phone and suggests a widget or an automation.
Voice and memory
Speak a task instead of typing it, and the agent remembers what you told it, such as your gym days or your usual café.

Two editions

Both run the agent inside the app, in a small Linux system that downloads about 130 MB when you choose to set it up. Each agent needs your own account with its provider.
